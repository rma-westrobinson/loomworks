Green Row Farm Market sells local vegetables and fruit out of Simcoe, Ontario, year round. No off-season, no shortened winter hours. Just fresh produce, seven days a week.

Why people drive out for it

  • Produce sourced locally in the surrounding county rather than shipped in from a distribution warehouse.
